Skip hire in Sanctuary Point

Sanctuary Point is one of the bigger residential areas in the Bay & Basin, with St Georges Basin, Basin View and Erowal Bay nearby. It's mostly suburban housing on standard blocks, so jobs tend to be household clean-outs, renovations and big garden tidy-ups. A 3 m³ skip suits a garage or yard tidy, and 6 m³ a kitchen or a full backyard overhaul.

Green waste after storms

Big gums and coastal scrub mean lots of branches and leaves after wind and storms. A green-only skip is usually cheaper than mixed waste, as long as you keep soil, plastic and rubbish out of it. Tree stumps and large logs may have size limits, so mention them in the form.

Nearest depots

The closest Shoalhaven City Council depots are the Huskisson Recycling & Waste Depot at 235 Huskisson Road and the Sussex Inlet Recycling & Waste Depot at 40 The Springs Road, Sussex Inlet. Check council's website for each depot's hours and fees. Asbestos goes to West Nowra or Ulladulla, by appointment only.

Council rules

On your own driveway or lawn, approval generally isn't needed. On the road or nature strip, check with Shoalhaven City Council. See the permit guide.
